Library Rules & Regulations

• 1. ID Card is Compulsory: Students must carry their LPU ID Card for entry in the library.

• 2. Maintain Silence: Talking loudly, talking on phone and making noise is not allowed inside the library.

• 3. Seat Booking: One student can book only one seat at a time. Do not sit on a seat without booking.

• 4. Reach on Time: Students must reach the library within 15 minutes of booking time, otherwise the seat will be auto-cancelled.

• 5. Food is Not Allowed: Eating food, cold drinks or tea is not allowed inside the library. Only water bottle is allowed.

• 6. Take Care of Books: Do not tear, write or take books outside without issuing. Fine will be charged.

• 7. Mobile on Silent Mode: Keep your mobile on silent or vibration mode. Video calls are strictly not allowed.

• 8. Keep Cleanliness: Keep your seat clean and throw waste in the dustbin.

• 9. 2 Books Limit: One student can issue maximum 2 books for 14 days at a time.

• 10. Late Fine: Rs. 5/- per day fine will be charged for late return of books.
